Our Story · Source before flavor

Tea is never just a tasting note.

Every blend begins with a landscape, a season, and the hands that understand both. Teadrops exists to keep those origins visible from the field to the final cup.

Where the rows begin

Thái Nguyên, told through its harvest.

In northern Vietnam, mountain air and carefully tended tea rows shape leaves with a deep, malty character. Our Peach Black Tea begins there: leaves are picked by hand, processed in small batches, and paired with dried peach only after the tea has developed its own voice. We share the place and process behind each ingredient because provenance should be part of the experience—not fine print beneath it.

01 · Place

Picked where it grows

We begin with the conditions that shape a tea: elevation, climate, harvest timing, and the knowledge of the people working among the rows.

02 · Process

Made visible

From handpicking and withering to rolling and drying, we explain the decisions that create a tea's flavor instead of reducing it to a label.

03 · Blend

Peach, with restraint

Dried peach adds brightness without covering the black tea beneath it. The blend is designed to reveal its origin, not disguise it.

Our promise
Keep the grower in the story, the process in view, and the origin present in every cup.

The Story Behind This Tea

From the Thái Nguyên Mountains

Nestled in the misty highlands of northern Vietnam, the Nguyễn family has cultivated tea for five generations. Their black tea leaves are hand-rolled and sun-withered using methods passed down through the decades, then paired with peach pieces dried on stone slabs at the farm's edge.
