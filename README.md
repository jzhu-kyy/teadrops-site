# Teadrops — Peach Black Tea

An interactive e-commerce concept for Teadrops' Peach Black Tea launch. The experience follows a complete flow from discovery and product browsing to product detail, add-ons, and cart.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder with any static web server.

## Homepage direction

- Full-bleed Vietnamese tea garden hero
- Oil-painted, canvas-textured visual treatment
- Subtle animated harvesting layers and pointer parallax
- Editorial typography with earthy greens and peach accents
- Reduced-motion support for accessibility

## Pages and interactions

- Home and featured teas
- Filterable tea collection
- Peach Black Tea product detail
- Bundle and add-on selection
- Functional cart and quantity controls

See [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md) for the design rationale and AI-use disclosure.
